>SYNTHETIC DATA RATIONALE
Real annotated road datasets are scarce in Ethiopia due to limited sensor infrastructure. This system generates 1000 synthetic records using validated weather, crack-type, tire, and vehicle load factors to approximate real-world conditions for model training and testing. Each record is uniquely identified by a combination of road, crack type, vehicle, weather, tire condition, speed, temperature, road age and traffic volume — no two rows are identical.
